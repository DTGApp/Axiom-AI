Privacy Policy — Second Sense

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Short version: Second Sense collects nothing, sends nothing, and asks for no permissions. It has no internet code in it at all.

Information collected

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It requests no Android permissions, contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and no sign-in of any kind. It does not read your contacts, location, files, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small preferences file on the device itself, containing only:

  • your best solo accuracy score and a count of rounds played;
  • your sound, music, and haptics on/off settings.

This never leaves your device. You can erase it with the in-app Reset stats button, or by uninstalling the app. If you have Android Backup enabled, Android itself may include this file in your own Google account backup; the developer has no access to it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
