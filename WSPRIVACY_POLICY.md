# Privacy Policy – Whatsapp Scheduler

Last updated: [03-Oct-2026]

**What the app does:** it sends messages you schedule through WhatsApp at the times you choose.

**Data we collect:** none. Your schedules, phone numbers, messages and any attached photos or videos are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not have servers and do not receive your data.

**Accessibility Service:** used only to tap the Send button in WhatsApp for messages you scheduled. It does not read, record, store or transmit screen content, and is active only on WhatsApp windows.

**Contacts:** if you pick a contact, only the selected number and name are read, and they are stored on your device only.

**Sharing:** we do not share, sell or transmit any data to third parties.

**Deleting data:** delete a schedule in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

**Not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.**
