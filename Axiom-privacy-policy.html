For any privacy-related questions, contact us at the address listed at the bottom of this page.

02 —

The Core Architecture

Axiom has no INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. This is not a policy choice — it is a technical constraint enforced by the Android operating system. Axiom is physically incapable of transmitting data over a network connection. You can verify this yourself using any Android package analyser.

All data generated by Axiom is stored in your device's private filesDir — a sandboxed location inaccessible to other applications without root access. When you delete the app, all data is permanently deleted with it.

03 —

What Data Axiom Processes Locally

The following data is processed entirely on your device to generate insights. None of it is transmitted, shared, or accessible to us.

App usage patterns
Which apps you use, for how long, and at what time of day. Collected via Android's UsageStats API. We see time spent — never content inside apps.
Notification activity signals
Presence of notifications from apps — used to infer activity patterns. We never read notification content, titles, or text.
Calendar events (optional)
Event timing and duration only — to understand your schedule rhythm. We never read event titles, descriptions, attendees, or locations.
Health & activity data (optional)
Step counts and sleep duration via Android Health Connect — if you grant access. Used only to correlate with phone habits. Never shared.
Your stated goals and reflections
What you type in the onboarding flow and companion chat. Stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database. Never transmitted anywhere.
Dream logs and weekly feedback
Your responses to Axiom's weekly check-ins and dream cycle logs. Stored locally only. Used to calibrate your personal baseline.

05 —

Android Permissions Explained

Axiom requests the minimum permissions necessary to function. Each permission is explained below with its exact purpose.

PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Core feature — reads which apps you use and for how long. All processing on-device. This is Axiom's primary data source.
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER Detects activity signals from notification presence only. Content is never read.
READ_CALENDAR Optional. Reads event timing to understand your daily rhythm. Titles and details are never accessed.
READ_HEALTH_DATA (Health Connect) Optional. Reads step count and sleep duration only if you choose to connect Health Connect.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required to deliver proactive insights and nudges at meaningful moments. Android 13+ only.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restarts background services after your device reboots so Axiom continues learning without manual action.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps the pattern detection engine running in the background on older Android versions.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Ensures the nightly Dream Cycle analysis runs reliably without being killed by battery management.
06 —

The Life File — Your Encrypted Backup

Axiom offers an optional encrypted backup called the Life File. This is an AES-256-CBC encrypted archive of your complete Axiom history, protected by a password that only you know.

When you export a Life File, it is saved to your device and shared via your chosen app (iCloud, Google Drive, email) — entirely under your control. Axiom does not receive, store, or have access to the Life File or its contents. We do not store your password. If you lose it, the file cannot be recovered.

This is intentional. Your data is yours. We are not the custodian of it.

07 —

Third-Party Services

Axiom contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app uses the following open-source libraries, none of which transmit data:

Room (AndroidX) Local SQLite database management. On-device only.
WorkManager (AndroidX) Background task scheduling. No network component used.
Gson (Google) JSON serialisation for local storage. No network component.

Axiom is distributed via Google Play. Google's standard Play Store privacy practices apply to the download and installation process. Axiom has no control over or access to data collected by Google Play itself.

08 —

Children's Privacy

Axiom is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly process data from children. As Axiom collects no data on any server, there is no mechanism by which we could receive or store a child's data.

If you are under 16, please do not use Axiom without parental consent.

09 —

Your Rights

Because all data stays on your device, you already exercise full control over it at all times. There is no data to request, correct, or delete from our systems — we have none.

You can delete all Axiom data permanently at any time by:

In-app reset Profile → Reset All Data. Permanently wipes all local records.
Uninstalling the app Android deletes all app data from filesDir on uninstall.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) are entitled to rights under GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and portability. Because we hold no personal data, these rights are satisfied by the architecture itself. For any queries, contact us below.
