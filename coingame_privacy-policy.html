Privacy Policy

Coin Clash — Android app

Last updated: September 19, 2026

✓ Coin Clash does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Overview

Coin Clash is an offline, pass-and-play dice game played entirely on one device. This policy explains — because app stores require every app to have one — what data the app does and does not handle. In short: it doesn't handle any.

Information We Collect

None. Coin Clash does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or diagnostic information. The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no user profile of any kind.

Internet Access

Coin Clash does not request internet access and makes no network connections. All game logic, artwork, and sound run entirely on your device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions (no camera, location, contacts, storage, microphone, etc.). It only needs what Android grants every app by default to draw its screen and play its own bundled sound files.

Third-Party Services

Coin Clash contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. There are no third parties to share data with, because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's Privacy

Coin Clash is safe for users of all ages. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either, and is compliant with COPPA and similar children's privacy regulations by design.

Data Retention & Deletion

There is nothing to retain or delete. Game state (coin counts, whose turn it is) lives only in memory while the app is open and resets when you restart a game or close the app. Nothing persists to a server, and nothing is backed up to any account.

Changes to This Policy

If Coin Clash ever changes what it collects (for example, if a future update adds optional cloud saves or analytics), this page will be updated first and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
